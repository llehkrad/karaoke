Anatomy/MelodyHub dashboard

Design elements & colour breakdown

A three-column dark-rail dashboard: near-black navigation, near-white workspace, one violet accent doing all the work, and photography carrying the emotion. Everything below is sampled from the screenshot.

One geometric humanist sans across the whole UI at three weights (400 / 500 / 700). Emphasis comes from italic and colour, never from a second family — except the single handwritten line laid over the hero photo.

04 — Structure & geometry

Three zones
Fixed dark rail (~240px), fluid work column, fixed context column (~380px). A dark player bar pins the full width.
Radius ladder
8px icon squares → 12px cards → 16px hero & panels → full pill for buttons, chips and avatars.
8px rhythm
24px card padding, 16px gutters, 32–40px between sections. Cards use a 1px #EEF0F5 hairline plus a barely-there shadow — depth by contrast, not elevation.
Icon treatment
Thin-stroke line icons, ~1.5px, in a tinted rounded square. Navigation icons sit unboxed at 20px in muted grey, white when active.

06 — Imagery & voice

Photography
Low-key, night-lit music scenes with violet and magenta bokeh. Always darkened enough for white type to clear 4.5:1, gradient scrimmed left to right.
Motif
A four-bar violet equaliser stands in for the logo and reappears on dark panels. Short underscore rules close each quote block.
Copy voice
Warm, three-beat fragments — “Ideas. People. Progress.” Every card pairs a plain noun title with an encouraging one-liner underneath.
